Molly Taylor

I recently graduated from Princeton University, where I majored in computer science and minored in history.

On campus, I led the features section of The Daily Princetonian, worked as a Writing Center Fellow, and contributed to research at the Center for Digital Humanities.

I currently work as a data visualization developer in Boston, MA. My data journalism has been published in the Financial Times and OpenSecrets.
